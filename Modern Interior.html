About Us

Design that fits the way you live and work

Modern Interior is an interior design and interior solutions company based in DHA Phase 6, Karachi. We work with homeowners, businesses and hospitality owners to turn ideas into well-planned, beautifully finished spaces.

Our approach begins with listening. We study the space, your needs and your budget, then develop layouts, materials and details that feel considered, practical and lasting.

CreativityFresh, tailored ideas for every brief.
QualityGood materials and careful workmanship.
FunctionalitySpaces that work as well as they look.
